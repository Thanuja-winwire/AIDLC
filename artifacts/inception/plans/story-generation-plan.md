# Story Generation Plan

## Approach
Simple workflow: feature-based breakdown, one or more INVEST stories per functional requirement (FR-1..FR-11), two personas (Admin, Staff), Given/When/Then-style acceptance criteria. The specification already resolves scope, roles and permissions, so no further questions are needed.

## Story Breakdown Options Considered
- User journey-based: good for flows, but overlaps heavily across roles
- **Feature-based (chosen)**: maps directly to FR-1..FR-11 and traceability
- Persona-based: duplicates stories across Admin/Staff
- Epic-based: unnecessary for a single unit

## Questions
None. Persona, granularity and acceptance-criteria format are fully determined by the approved specification.
