# Personas

## Task Owner (single user)
- **Role**: Individual who tracks personal or work tasks; the only user of the app (no login).
- **Goals**: Capture tasks quickly, keep priorities and due dates visible, see overall progress at a glance.
- **Pain points**: Scattered to-dos, no quick view of what is pending or high priority.
- **Tech comfort**: Comfortable with common web apps.
- **Mapped stories**: US-1 to US-7
