# Personas

## Persona 1: Casey, the Subscriber (Customer)
- **Role:** `customer`
- **Profile:** Individual or small-team user who subscribes to a plan for a product.
- **Goals:** Find a suitable plan, subscribe quickly, change plans, keep track of invoices and payments, cancel without friction.
- **Frustrations:** Surprise charges, unclear billing dates, hard-to-find invoices.
- **Key features:** Plans, subscription management, invoices, payment history, payment method.

## Persona 2: Avery, the Administrator (Admin)
- **Role:** `admin`
- **Profile:** Operations/finance staff running the subscription business.
- **Goals:** Maintain the plan catalogue, monitor revenue and churn, investigate customer billing issues, trigger renewals when needed.
- **Frustrations:** Lack of visibility into failed payments and churn drivers.
- **Key features:** Analytics dashboard, plan management, customer/subscription lookup, all invoices/payments.

## Persona 3: Jordan, the Struggling Customer (Customer with failed payments)
- **Role:** `customer`
- **Profile:** Subscriber whose card is declined or has insufficient funds, sometimes at renewal.
- **Goals:** Understand why a payment failed, fix the payment method, recover the subscription (past_due) without losing history.
- **Frustrations:** Unclear failure reasons, no obvious way to retry.
- **Key features:** Payment history with failure reasons, payment method update, manual "renew now" retry.
