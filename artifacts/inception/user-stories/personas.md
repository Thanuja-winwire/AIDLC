# Personas

## Alex Morgan — Admin (Inventory Manager)
- **Role**: Admin
- **Goals**: Keep the catalog, suppliers and locations accurate; control purchasing; oversee stock health and sales performance.
- **Frustrations**: Stock discrepancies, unapproved purchasing, no audit trail.
- **Permissions**: Full access, including users, products, categories, locations, suppliers, PO creation/approval.

## Sam Rivera — Staff (Warehouse / Sales Associate)
- **Role**: Staff
- **Goals**: Quickly receive deliveries, move stock, correct counts and record sales.
- **Frustrations**: Slow screens, unclear errors, not knowing what is low on stock.
- **Permissions**: View catalog/suppliers/POs; receive POs; adjust, transfer, sell; view alerts and reports. Cannot manage master data, users or approve POs.
