# Personas

## Inventory Manager
- **Role**: Sole user of the system; open access, no login or roles.
- **Responsibilities**: Maintains the product catalogue, warehouses and suppliers; raises and receives purchase orders; moves and corrects stock; monitors alerts, history and reports.
- **Goals**: Always know what is in stock and where; avoid stock-outs; keep an auditable trail of every movement.
- **Pain points**: Spreadsheets drift out of date; stock errors go unnoticed; hard to see which items need reordering.
- **Needs**: Fast search, clear validation messages, dashboard overview, exportable reports.
