# Requirements Clarification Questions

## Question 1
How should multiple locations (needed for stock transfers) be modelled?

A) Multiple warehouses/locations, with stock tracked per location

B) Single location only; transfers are just logged records

C) Other (please describe after [Answer]: tag below)

[Answer]: A) Multiple warehouses/locations, with stock tracked per location

## Question 2
How should sales data for the sales reports be captured?

A) Built-in sales orders/transactions recorded in this system (stock decreases on sale)

B) Reports derived only from stock-out adjustments

C) Other (please describe after [Answer]: tag below)

[Answer]: A) Built-in sales orders/transactions recorded in this system (stock decreases on sale)

## Question 3
What should the Admin and Staff role split be?

A) Admin: full access including users, suppliers, and approving POs. Staff: view, stock adjustments, transfers, receiving POs, and recording sales

B) Admin: everything. Staff: read-only

C) Other (please describe after [Answer]: tag below)

[Answer]: A) Admin: full access including users, suppliers, and approving POs. Staff: view, stock adjustments, transfers, receiving POs, and recording sales
